% ================================================================ LEFT: why
\column{0.55}

\block{\sect{1.}{Background}}{\bodylarge
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Prevention is a major factor in reducing disease onset; modifying 14 risk factors could prevent or delay up to 45\% of dementia cases worldwide.\textsuperscript{1}
    \item In addition to activation through specific cognitive exercises, physical activity (such as walking) is crucially important, as maintaining physical activity protects against dementia.
    \item Prevention needs tools that reach people \textbf{before symptoms}, at population scale: risk screening, cognitive testing and lifestyle support.
    \item Mobile apps can do this, but \textbf{keeping users engaged} long term is the main barrier in digital health.
  \end{itemize}
}

\block{\sect{2.}{Objective}}{\bodylarge
  \begin{itemize}
    \item To evaluate user uptake and long-term engagement with the Terrapino mobile app.
    \item To characterize the data streams generated by the platform.
    \item To demonstrate how the app supports dementia risk screening, cognitive training, and healthy lifestyle habits.
  \end{itemize}
}

% ================================================================ RIGHT: how
\column{0.45}

\block{\sect{3.}{Methods}}{\bodylarge
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Design:} retrospective analysis of anonymized real-world data from \textbf{38,411} Czech- and English-speaking users over 3 years.
    \item \textbf{What the app measures and trains:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{ARA score:} Alzheimer's Risk Assessment questionnaire on modifiable risk factors, physical activity and diet.
      \item \textbf{Harrison BASIC:} repeatable episodic-memory test (Brief And Simple Index of Cognition, with Prof.\ John Harrison).
      \item \textbf{Emotion recognition} task (social cognition).
      \item \textbf{Daily training:} 7 cognitive mini-games and a step tracker for walking.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Motivation:} points for activity, redeemable for partner offers.
    \item \textbf{Outcomes:} uptake, engagement, risk categories, cognitive test results.
  \end{itemize}
}

\end{columns}

\block{}{\bodylarge
  \begin{minipage}[t]{0.38\linewidth}\vspace{0pt}\raggedright
    \panelhead{\sect{5.}{Conclusion}}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Target population reach:} a gamified app reached 38,411 users, two thirds of them in the 50--80 age group that prevention targets.
      \item \textbf{Risk is visible early:} one in three risk-assessed users had an increased-or-higher risk profile, and 41\% of tested users scored below normal on memory.
      \item \textbf{Users engage:} highly active users spend over 10 minutes per session; points-based rewards are a practical engagement lever.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{minipage}\hfill
  \begin{minipage}[t]{0.34\linewidth}\vspace{0pt}\raggedright
    \panelhead{Next steps}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Longitudinal analysis of repeated Harrison BASIC scores and long-term retention.
      \item Predictive models and personalized lifestyle recommendations.
      \item Physician reports that bring app data into clinical care.
      \item More language versions.
    \end{itemize}
